The 100 MW long Cinergy–PJM-W spread earned approximately $383k over the two-week period. Cinergy strengthened relative to PJM-W (or equivalently, PJM-W weakened). From a basis-risk perspective, the P&L of this long spread effectively offsets the margin impact of “selling power in PJM-W and buying power in Cinergy.” In other words, it locks in the future cross-hub price difference at the entry-level (around 7.575 $/MWh), making the hedge theoretically very effective. Workings: the original mids are 47.625 $/MWh at Cinergy and 55.20 $/MWh at PJM-W (initial spread 7.575), the new mids are 49.125 and 54.125 (new spread 5.00), and over Jul–Aug 2002 baseload (62 days × 24 h = 1,488 h at 100 MW) the long Cinergy leg gains 1.50 $/MWh or $223,200 and the short PJM-W leg gains 1.075 $/MWh or $159,960, for a combined $383,160.
