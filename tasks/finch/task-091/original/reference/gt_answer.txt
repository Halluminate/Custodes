The 100 MW long Cinergy–PJM-W spread earned approximately $383k over the two-week period. Cinergy strengthened relative to PJM-W (or equivalently, PJM-W weakened). From a basis-risk perspective, the P&L of this long spread effectively offsets the margin impact of “selling power in PJM-W and buying power in Cinergy.” In other words, it locks in the future cross-hub price difference at the entry-level (around 7.575 $/MWh), making the hedge theoretically very effective.
